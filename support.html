Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use these apps?

No account is required. All three apps are designed around records stored and used on your device.

Where can I download or update an app?

Visit the Products & Services page, choose an app, and follow its App Store link to download or update it.

Where can I find an app’s privacy information?

See the app’s App Store listing for privacy information and the privacy policy link provided by the app.

Does MemoNest support cloud sync?

MemoNest focuses on local notes. Its current App Store description does not offer cloud sync or collaboration.
